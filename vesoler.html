How to play safely

The rules

  1. It's fiction. Royce Technologies Samsara, the city of Samsara, and everyone in them are made up.
  2. Everything is online. Nothing is hidden in the real world, so never go anywhere to look.
  3. Nothing needs hacking. Every answer can be found without breaking into anything. If it feels like you're forcing a lock, you're on the wrong path.
  4. Never contact real people or real companies about the game, including real businesses that share a name with something in it.
  5. Share what you find. The game is built so nobody can solve it alone.

Before you start

Content notes

  • Flashing images: some videos and games have brief flashes. Each one is marked in its description.
  • Unsettling audio and video: loud noises, sudden cuts, distortion, and dead air.
  • Medical coercion and surveillance: a company that measures people without their knowledge.
